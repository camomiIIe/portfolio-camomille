## Concept

Made to introduce RD Station's Partner Program to agencies and consultants across Latin America, in Spanish. The opening shot sets the tone — two hands reaching toward each other across a solid circle, a nod to partnership itself — before the film moves into fragmented, reassembled frames and bold typographic callouts, all in color-blocked collage over black-and-white footage.
