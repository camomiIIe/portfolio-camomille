## Concept

RD Station's YouTube channel had just crossed 50,000 subscribers — a number that's easy to state and hard to make anyone feel. Instead of a straightforward thank-you, the film goes looking for "50" everywhere else it shows up, playing each detour straight before cutting to the next.
